Questions

Who owns the code?

You get the full source in your own private repository. The shared core stays ours and is licensed to you for your app (non-exclusive, no resale as a template). Features built only for you are yours.

What if I need something that isn't on the list?

We quote it as a new module before work starts. It is built into the same core, so it works with everything else.

Can my users use it in English or another language?

Yes. Interface text is set per app as part of the build. The modules themselves are language-independent.

Which payment processor do you use?

Payments are charged through your own processor account, so the money goes straight to you. Stripe support is being added; tell us your country and processor and we confirm before quoting.

Is there a demo I can try?

Yes. After the questionnaire we set up a demo with the modules you picked and sample data.

Where are you and how do we talk?

We are a small studio in South Korea. Communication is in English by email or your project chat, with replies within one business day (KST).

Tell us what you need

Which modules, who the users are, and roughly when you want to launch. We reply with the questionnaire and a price range.
